Use HttpCfg (see https://msdn.microsoft.com/en-us/library/windows/desktop/aa364478%28v=vs.85%29.aspx?f=255&MSPPError=-2147217396)

or http://www.stevestechspot.com/HttpConfigUpdate.aspx

Add an SSL certificate for 127.0.0.1 on your chosen port.

See also http://ib-krajewski.blogspot.co.uk/2015/09/https-support-for-casablanca-server-and.html


netsh http add sslcert ipport=0.0.0.0:11238 certhash=<CertificateThumbprintWithNoSpaces> appid={790C242A-DAA6-46FF-8E04-B1EB280F3BA2}

NOTE: Also watch out for the special character that the certificate manager inserts at the front of the thumbprint.

To be able to run as non-admin:
add urlacl url=https://yoururl:port/ user=DOMAIN\user listen=yes

Your username is probably YourMachineName\YourUsername


Finally: add the port via the advanced firewall tool.
